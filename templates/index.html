No-website lead finder

1. Search an area

Pulls listings from Google. Keeps everyone — businesses with no website are "new site" leads, businesses with a website are "revamp" leads. This costs a small amount on your Google Places API billing per search.

2. Leads

Google doesn't expose email addresses, so add them yourself as you find them (call, check their Facebook page, etc). Use the "find" link to search for it quickly.

3. Send a campaign

Sends only to selected leads that have an email and haven't unsubscribed. Every email gets a footer with your sender details and an unsubscribe link, required by law in most places.

Use {{business_name}} in any message, and {{website_url}} when messaging the "Has website" group.
